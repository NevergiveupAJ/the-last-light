# THE LAST LIGHT — Game Design

## Genre
First-person survival horror / exploration. Designed for short mobile play sessions with touch controls.

## Premise
At 2:17 AM, a forest research station loses power. Alex wakes alone inside the abandoned zone. The emergency gate is locked. Three power fuses are scattered through the maze, and a creature known as **The Hollow** hunts by sound and proximity.

## Main objective
1. Explore the research zone.
2. Find and interact with all three fuses.
3. Keep distance from The Hollow and manage flashlight power.
4. Reach the emergency gate after restoring power.

## Main characters
- **Alex** — the player character; currently represented by the first-person viewpoint in the prototype.
- **The Hollow** — a tall, distorted forest entity that patrols, investigates activity, pursues the player, and attacks at close range.

## Controls
- Touch buttons: move/turn, flashlight, sprint, interact.
- Keyboard prototype: WASD or arrow keys to move/turn, Shift to sprint, F for flashlight, E to interact.

## Core systems in prototype
- Maze exploration and collision
- Three collectible fuse objectives
- Flashlight battery management
- Monster threat and health
- Touch-first mobile controls
- Win/lose screens and replay

## Planned production features
- Replace placeholder visuals with a consistent original 3D or 2.5D art direction.
- Add authored character/monster animations and sound cues.
- Add settings, pause menu, subtitles, sensitivity controls, and accessibility options.
- Add checkpoints/save data and additional levels.
- Add original soundtrack and effects; generate or compose them specifically for this game and confirm rights before release.
- Add optional cosmetic store only after inventory, purchase validation, refund handling, and platform billing are implemented.

## Scope note
The prototype demonstrates the core loop; planned features above are not represented as already completed.
