# Story and Gameplay Bible

## Premise
At 2:17 AM, Alex wakes inside an abandoned forest research station with a damaged radio and a dying flashlight.
A storm has cut power to the emergency gate. Three industrial fuses are missing. A creature called The Hollow
moves through the station and reacts to movement, light, and noise.

## Main objective
1. Explore the forest entrance and find the maintenance map.
2. Search the cabins for a battery and a medical kit.
3. Enter the generator station and recover Fuse A.
4. Reach the research facility and recover Fuse B.
5. Find Fuse C in the underground maintenance tunnels.
6. Install all three fuses at the emergency gate control box.
7. Trigger the gate, survive the final chase, and escape.

## Story beats
- Opening: emergency lights flicker; radio repeats “Do not open the lower door.”
- Act I — The Perimeter: learn movement, flashlight battery, crouching, and hiding.
- Act II — The Generator: first close encounter; power surges reveal The Hollow.
- Act III — The Research Wing: discover audio logs explaining the facility's evacuation.
- Act IV — The Tunnels: darkness, scarce supplies, and a route that forces a risky choice.
- Finale — The Gate: the gate opens slowly, triggering a final chase.
- Ending: Alex escapes, but the radio receives a new transmission from inside the forest.

## Optional collectibles
- Audio log 01: “Night Shift”
- Lab note: “Acoustic response trials”
- Torn map: tunnel access route
- Photograph: the facility before abandonment
- Radio recording: final evacuation call

## Core survival loops
Explore -> hear a clue -> search -> manage battery/stamina -> avoid or distract the monster -> collect objective -> unlock route.

## Difficulty modes
Story: slower monster, more supplies.
Survival: standard monster and supplies.
Nightmare: longer chase, scarce batteries, faster detection.

## Enemy rules
- Patrol between authored waypoints.
- Running and loud interactions can attract it.
- A flare distracts it briefly; it is not a permanent solution.
- Line of sight and navigation checks should be implemented for final AI.
- Attacks need cooldowns, hit animations, audio cues, and clear recovery time.

## Weapons / defensive tools
The flashlight is a navigation tool, not a gun.
- Flare: one-use distraction and brief illumination.
- Survival knife: emergency close-range defense with durability; does not reliably kill the monster.
- Wooden pry tool: opens some boarded containers; slow and noisy.
For horror tension, prioritize escape and stealth over combat.
