# Art Bible and Copyable Asset Prompts

## Global style
Photorealistic 3D survival horror, physically based materials, damp surfaces, cold moonlight, volumetric fog,
limited warm practical lights, believable proportions, cinematic but readable silhouettes, no existing franchise
characters, no logos from other games. Keep models game-ready and optimized for mobile.

## Alex — character model prompt
"Create an original game-ready 3D survivor named Alex for a first-person survival horror game. Adult human,
weatherproof dark olive jacket, worn charcoal cargo trousers, mud-stained hiking boots, compact emergency
backpack, radio clipped to strap, flashlight in right hand. Provide front, side, back turnaround, neutral A-pose,
clean silhouette, realistic PBR materials, separate mesh parts, 8k texture source but optimized game textures,
humanoid skeleton suitable for retargeting, no text, no watermark."

## The Hollow — monster prompt
"Create an original game-ready horror creature called The Hollow. Tall, thin, asymmetrical humanoid silhouette,
long forearms, crooked shoulders, pale weathered skin, torn dark fabric, obscured face and subtle amber eye glow.
Provide front, side, back turnaround, neutral rigging pose, believable anatomy distortion, PBR roughness and
normal maps, humanoid skeleton with extra spine/neck controls, separate materials, animation-ready, no gore,
no text, no watermark, no resemblance to known horror franchises."

## Forest environment prompt
"Build an original abandoned research station in a dense pine forest at night, wet ground, fog banks, broken
chain-link fence, old warning lights, fallen branches, distant laboratory windows, moonlight, modular game
environment, separate reusable assets, collision-friendly geometry, mobile-friendly polygon budget, PBR materials."

## Research lab prompt
"Original abandoned forest research laboratory interior for a survival horror game: flickering fluorescent lights,
rusted cabinets, old terminals, cable trays, stained concrete, overturned chairs, paper files, red emergency lamps,
locked doors, believable floor plan, modular walls and props, optimized for real-time mobile rendering."

## Weapons and item prompts
- Flashlight: "Realistic worn aluminum handheld flashlight, scratched body, glass lens, physically based material, game prop, clean isolated turnaround."
- Flare: "Emergency red flare prop, compact safety grip, bright emissive tip, game-ready low-poly and high-poly reference."
- Medkit: "Worn first-aid pouch with medical cross, faded fabric, zipper, isolated prop reference."
- Fuse: "Industrial ceramic power fuse with metal contacts, isolated game collectible prop."
- Gate control: "Weathered industrial fuse control box beside a sliding emergency gate, labels as separate decals, game-ready prop."

## UI prompt
"Design a readable mobile survival horror HUD: minimal health and stamina bars, flashlight battery indicator,
three-fuse objective tracker, interact prompt, clean high-contrast typography, landscape layout, no clutter,
buttons large enough for thumbs, original icons."

## Store icon prompt
"Create a square 1024x1024 store icon for THE LAST LIGHT, original abandoned forest silhouette, a small flashlight beam,
two glowing eyes far between trees, restrained blue-black palette with one warm light, bold readable composition,
no tiny text, no existing game logo."

## Store screenshot prompt
"Create a cinematic mobile game store screenshot for THE LAST LIGHT showing a survivor with a flashlight facing
a tall shadow creature outside an abandoned research station at night, realistic 3D graphics, mist, readable safe
space for a short caption, original assets, no watermark."

## Audio prompts
- Ambience: "Loopable night forest wind, distant branches creaking, light rain on metal roofing, low electrical hum; no speech."
- Monster proximity: "Subtle layered low breathing and irregular wet footsteps, spatialized, grows louder with proximity; no music."
- Chase cue: "Original tense percussion and bowed-metal texture that builds dynamically, no melody copied from existing works."
- UI: "Short restrained analog click for pickup, low thump for objective completion, soft radio static for new clue."
