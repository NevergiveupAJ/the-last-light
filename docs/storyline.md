# Storyline

**Premise.** At 2:17 AM, Alex wakes inside the abandoned Northwood research station deep in a pine forest. A storm has cut the power, the emergency gate on the north fence is locked, and three industrial fuses are missing. Something called **The Hollow** moves between the trees. It reacts to movement, light and sound.

**Beats as built in the game**
1. *Forest Entrance*: a wrecked truck, a broken fence, and a torn maintenance map on a crate (it marks all three fuses).
2. *Cabins*: supplies, and a photograph of the staff with one face scratched out.
3. *Generator Station*: Fuse A and Audio Log 01, "Night Shift".
4. *Research Facility*: Fuse B in the back server room, plus the "Acoustic Response Trials" lab note.
5. *Underground Tunnels*: Fuse C near the sealed lower door, plus the final evacuation radio call.
6. *Emergency Gate*: insert three fuses. The gate grinds open slowly and The Hollow comes for you. Run.

**Endings**
- **The last light goes out**: The Hollow catches Alex.
- **Escape**: Alex reaches the road at dawn. The radio picks up a new transmission from inside the forest.
- **True ending, "The Lower Door"**: escape with all five documents. The scratched-out face was the night-shift technician who opened the lower door. The Hollow used to answer to that name.

The document texts live in `scripts/game_manager.gd` under `DOCUMENTS`.
